## What I Track

![[what_I_track.png]]

This list has been honed over time. Everything here has been tracked since at least January 1st 2020, and several items date back much earlier. I track these because they’ve proven useful - or at least amusing enough to keep noting

- **Workouts**
    - `Name` - of the workout (or short description)
    - `Type` - one of strength/cardio/mobility
    - `Note` - comment field, usually about how the workout went
- **Pains**
    - `Pains` - comma separated list of symptoms
    - `Meds` - comma separated list of medicines/therapies
    - `Note` - what happened, or general place to complain
- **Media**
    - `Type` - one of book/movie/tv/videogame
    - `Title` - of the piece of media
    - `First Time` - yes or no, is this my first time with this piece of media?
- **Outings**
    - `Type` - comma separated list of various tags, e.g. eating out, seeing friends, etc
    - `Where` - you visited
    - `Note` - usually how it went or why we’re there
- **Quotes**
    - `Quote` - what was said
    - `Quoter` - who said it
    - `Note` - any additional context
- **Events**
    - `Event` - a catch-all for any time-based thing I want to make note of - *e.g. “replaced hot water heater”*
    - `Tag` - tags for later grouping events by type or topic
- **Doctor Visits**
    - `Type` - dentist, doctor, etc
    - `Who` - name of doctor
    - `Why` - reason you were there
    - `Notes` - take aways, reminders to self, etc
- **Daily Things**
    - Formulas summarizing all of the above (🤖)
    - `Summary` - A brief description of the day. What I did. Who I saw. How it went.
    - `Health` - 1-to-10 how healthy do I feel?
    - `Satisfaction` - 1-to-10 how satisfied am I with the day?
    - `Work Status` - one of Weekend/At Work/WFH/Holiday/Sick/Vacation
    - `Bedtime` - time of night falling asleep (🤖)
    - `Wake` - time of morning waking (🤖)
    - `Sleep Duration` - duration between the above (🤖)
    - `Sleep Location` - city & state I’m located at at 3:30 AM (🤖)
- **Weekly**
    - Formulas summarizing all of the above (🤖)
- **Monthly**
    - Formulas summarizing all of the above (🤖)
    - `Vacation` - any vacations taken
- **Quarterly**
    - Formulas summarizing all of the above (🤖)
    - `Fitness Test` - the date of the [Overall Fitness Test](https://aarongilly.com/Columns/379) I did during the quarter
- **Yearly**
    - Formulas summarizing all of the above (🤖)
    - `Big Event` - it seems like every year has one, they mark the passage of time
    - `Puzzle Box` - name of my [annual puzzle box](https://aaronspuzzles.com)

 > [!question] Items marked with (🤖) are populated automatically by formulas or scripts

## History

### Pre-history

It started, weirdly, with a [standup special by Demetri Martin](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4qkkQIzQ4VI) I saw in 2009.

![[demetri.jpeg]]

Demetri created a point system to help manage his life. After 27 weeks he concluded that *"you can't maximize a life"*. But the idea still sounded like what I needed. I came up with my own set of goals and started doing it myself.

![[my_list.jpeg]]

Melissa *(my then girlfriend, now wife)* had a time-tracking assignment in college. Every half hour for 168 hours she wrote down what she was doing & categorized it. She urged me to give it a shot to help manage the overwhelm and lack of direction I was feeling.

I combined both the time tracking and Demetri-Martin-esque goal tracking in something that looked like this:

![[Pasted image 20260306160730.png]]

This system worked, but took too much energy to maintain. After 17 weeks, in late 2010, I stopped.
### Data Journal Begins

When I graduated college I was, like most people, out on my own. For the first time in my life I had nobody watching over my shoulder to keep me healthy, productive, and generally not failing at life. That freaked me out.

Suddenly **having 100% of the responsibility for my own life was *scary***. I didn't know what to take care of, or what to pay attention to. I didn't want to wake up one day sad, alone, broke, and fat... but I didn't really know what to do.

> [!quote] 
> What gets measured gets managed - *Peter Drucker*

I thought back to the Demetri Martin video. I stopped last time because the pen & paper approach was too prone to failure. Lose the paper, not have the pen when you need it, I needed something else. Melissa suggested I create a Google Form for data input and use Google Sheets. I took her advice and was off to the races. I added a **daily journal entry** to the nightly tracking routine. I started tracking my bedtime and scored how I felt physically and felt about the day.

The first day of the new system was April 22nd, 2013. 

I continued, mostly unchanged, for 5 years.

In 2018 I no longer felt the point system was necessary - but everything else I'd began tracking continued to be useful. The nightly review of the day was a keystone habit. It is just a thing I do now.

I continued for another 8+ years (and counting).

While the form of my system changed *greatly* over the past decade, its function and its presence in my life remained the same. The effect it's had on me is impossible to measure, but I credit "this one simple habit" with a *lot*. 

Simply put:

> [!success] The Data Journal is — and continues to be — the most impactful project I’ve ever done.
