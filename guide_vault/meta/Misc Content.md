## Make Your Data Yours 

Your data is everywhere. Your phone knows how many steps you've taken. YouTube knows every video you've ever watched. Your credit card company knows how much you spent, when, and where. 

Yet you aren't really given the tools to really benefit from these data. They are data about you, but they are not really yours. You are limited to the tools they give you, working in the silos they've built, and dependent on them to continue to let you access your own data.

A data journal doesn't mean they don't have your data, but that you have it now too. And you aren't going to lose it.
