## Make Your Data Yours 

Your data is everywhere. Your phone knows how many steps you've taken. YouTube knows every video you've ever watched. Your credit card company knows how much you spent, when, and where. 

Yet you aren't really given the tools to really benefit from these data. They are data about you, but they are not really yours. You are limited to the tools they give you, working in the silos they've built, and dependent on them to continue to let you access your own data.

A data journal doesn't mean they don't have your data, but that you have it now too. And you aren't going to lose it. 

## Build Your Own

I think for this to truly "work" and bring long-term value **you need to build your own for yourself**. If you simply "adopt" a pre-packaged product I don't think you will have the sense of ownership and buy-in necessary to achieve the decades-long success... plus you hitch your wagon to a horse you don't fully control. Notion is an absolutely incredible platform, and you absolutely could use it to build your data journal - but you will be at risk of them changing their product or shutting their doors.

Because I build mine, I am fully bought in. It is a part of me and my evaluation of myself. I do not let this project fail, because it's mine. I do not "sit and wait" for new features to come along to fix things I don't like about it - I make it work exactly how I want.

This website is not about selling you a product, it's about planting the seed of an idea, and giving you thoughts, templates, and tools you can use or take inspiration from. 

You should centralize your own data.

1. Create a place for your data to go
2. Get data there
