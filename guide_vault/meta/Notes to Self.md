# Why

This thing I've built has been possibly the most impactful single project I've ever done in my life.

- Explain what a Data Journal is
- Explain why it benefits you
- Explain how you could build one of your own

I want to sell the concept and help someone make their own.
I don't want followers. I want independent people building their own way, and perhaps avoiding some mistakes I made in the process.
