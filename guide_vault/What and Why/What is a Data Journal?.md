# Overview

A Data Journal is a personal system for recording both what happens in your life and the data that surrounds it.

It combines journaling with structured tracking so that your days accumulate into something you can examine, learn from, and build on over time.

A Data Journal is ***not***:

- an app
- a subscription service
- a ready-made product

It's something you build and shape yourself, for yourself - to be used not for days or weeks, but for **life**.

# Details

A Data Journal combines:

1. **Reflection** — writing about your day
2. **Structured data** — tracking measurable aspects of life
3. **Time** — entries accumulating over *years*

Those three together create something unique.

> *Without reflection* → dashboard
> *Without structure* → diary
> *Without time* → fleeting notes
> 
> With all three → Data Journal

Data Journal is many things:

**Personal record**
- a time-indexed ledger of your life
- a collection of useful data you build yourself
- a repository of reference information about your past

**Tool for reflection**
- a place for journaling and self-reflection
- a way to see patterns in your behavior
- a source of clarity about how life is going

**System for growth**
- a habit tracker
- a system you can use for goal-setting
- a tool for intentional self-improvement

The real power of a Data Journal appears over long time horizons. Weeks turn into months, months into years, and eventually you accumulate a uniquely detailed record of your own life to learn from and use however you see fit.

## Minimum Viable Data Journal

As a tiny example, a minimal Data Journal could look like this:

| Date   | Journal                                                                                                                                                                                                 | Exercise   |
| ------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------- |
| 3/8/26 | Today I took it easy, after yesterday evening I wasn't feeling like doing much.                                                                                                                         | -          |
| 3/7/26 | Fun Saturday! The weather is surprisingly good, and that's been making me happy. I played basketball and went out for drinks with the team afterwards. Good times. Maybe too good.                      | Basketball |
| 3/6/26 | Ended the work week low on energy. Despite that, I still managed to go to the gym. Even though it wasn't my best exercise, it was just good to exercise the system. Saving some energy for the weekend. | Weights    |

This simple table contains the three core elements: a date, a journal entry, and a piece of structured data. Do this in a spreadsheet for a year and you'll have a much better picture of your life than most.

## More Realistic Data Journal

Over time, you'll probably want to add more things - perhaps growing beyond a single table.

![[example_data_journal.png]]

A more realistic Data Journal might include additional columns and tables for different time scales (for example, a "Days" sheet and a "Weeks" sheet). You may also have tables dedicated to particular types of things you want to track that don't fit well into the day/week/year-per-row organizational paradigm. [[The Reference Build - a Complete Data Journal Architecture|The Reference Build]] shows how this works.
