# Details

## Clarity

Data Journals are an excellent source of clarity.

- ✅ The very act of choosing what to keep track of is hugely clarifying
- ✅ Seeing how well you're adhering to your goals
- ✅ Noticing patterns in your behavior *alongside* the results they bring
- ✅ Remembering the past - when was the last time you saw *that one friend*

If you wake up and find your life is going really well (or really *not* well), you have the data to help see why - so you can help it go right more often.

## Consistency

Every productivity and fitness influencer worth their salt will inevitably stresses one message:

> [!tip] Consistency is key.

This adage comes in many forms, from many places:

- [Success is the product of daily habits — not once-in-a-lifetime transformations.](https://www.goodreads.com/quotes/9605362-success-is-the-product-of-daily-habits-not-once-in-a-lifetime-transformations)
- [We are what we repeatedly do. Excellence, then, is not an act but a habit.](https://www.goodreads.com/quotes/1003359-we-are-what-we-repeatedly-do-excellence-then-is-not)
- [Great works are performed not by strength, but perseverance; yonder palace was raised by single stones, yet you see its height and spaciousness.](https://www.goodreads.com/quotes/11239502-great-works-are-performed-not-by-strength-but-perseverance-yonder)
- [Success isn't always about greatness. It's about consistency.](https://www.goodreads.com/quotes/8579605-success-isn-t-always-about-greatness-it-s-about-consistency-consistent-hard)
- [Step by step, ferociously.](https://www.goodreads.com/quotes/11838457-bezos-was-methodical-his-motto-was-gradatim-ferociter-or-step)

Data Journals foster consistency.
